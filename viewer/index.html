Help: interpreting dependencies and timing
Read the arrows carefully. Requires / Wants: A → B pulls B into an activation transaction. After: A → B means A waits for B's ordering; Before means A precedes B. After alone does not start B. Requisite checks availability without starting it. PartOf propagates stop/restart, Conflicts is exclusion, and trigger relationships are separate. Current edges and timestamps do not reconstruct the complete cause or history of activation.
